New bundle

Select the content you want released if you miss a check-in. It will be encrypted in your browser before upload — the server only ever sees ciphertext. Small files (text, short videos, a handful of documents) work best; keep each bundle under 256 MiB.

What should I upload?
Any material you want released automatically if you stop checking in. A few practical rules:
  • Strip metadata first (EXIF on images, PDF properties, etc.). The release worker does not sanitize — what you upload is what goes out.
  • Name files neutrally. Filenames are readable to an operator during release.
  • If the material is extremely sensitive, encrypt it again with a PGP key or age recipient you trust, and include decryption instructions in a readme. Layer your crypto.
  • Under 256 MiB per bundle. For larger archives split across multiple bundles or stage a torrent link inside the bundle.

Before you upload

  • The encryption key is generated in your browser and never leaves until it's sealed under the server's release key.
  • Only the release pipeline can decrypt, and only when your policy state machine says so.
  • Operational note for whistle-blowers: use Tor Browser or a disposable VM. Do not include metadata you didn't mean to share — strip EXIF from images, check PDF properties, etc.
