Welcome.

You have an account. Before you arm anything, here's what the next few minutes look like.

You're going to do four things, in this order.

  1. Upload a bundle. Your files, encrypted in your browser before upload. You can upload more bundles later. Start with something small you'd be willing to lose if you make a configuration mistake.
  2. Add a destination. Where your bundle goes if a release fires — a public landing page, email recipients, or a webhook you control. Pick the least-consequential option you can for your first policy.
  3. Create a policy. How often you check in, how long the grace period is, which bundle(s) release to which destination(s).
  4. Arm the policy. The check-in countdown starts immediately. You can suspend or revoke at any time before it triggers.

A realistic starter policy

If you're unsure, here's a configuration most first-time users will find reasonable:

  • Check-in interval: 7 days
  • Grace period: 72 hours
  • Release mode: private (delivers to named recipients only)
  • Bundle: a short text note you're comfortable sharing with a trusted friend
  • Destination: email, addressed to yourself or a trusted recipient

Run this for a week or two. Check in on schedule. Miss a check-in deliberately to see the warning flow. Once you trust the tool, graduate to real material and a longer interval.

One more time: the things you cannot undo.

  • A release that has fired cannot be unpublished.
  • Lose both your passphrase and all 10 recovery codes — account is gone.
  • A policy in state released or revoked is terminal.

Everything else is safe to experiment with. Create drafts, revoke them, make new ones. Nothing before "arm" can hurt you.
