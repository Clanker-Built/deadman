New policy

A policy describes your check-in schedule and what happens if you miss. All fields can be edited later while the policy is still a draft. Arming the policy starts the check-in countdown immediately; you can suspend or revoke it at any time before it triggers.

Schedule
How do I pick an interval?
The interval is how often you must check in. Shorter = you surface a problem sooner, but also more likely to miss a check-in from normal life. Most users pick 3–14 days. Go longer if your life has natural gaps (travel, field work, hospital stays).
What is the grace period?
After your due date passes, you get this long to check in before the release fires. Warnings are sent throughout the grace window. Minimum 24 h. 72 h (3 days) is the sensible default for most users; 168 h (1 week) is reasonable if you might be offline.
What is a hold?
In a future version a trusted contact (not yet configurable) can pause the countdown for this long — useful if someone who knows you can vouch you're still alive after you missed a check-in but before releasing. Set 0 for now.
Release mode
Which mode should I pick?
Start with private. Live with the tool for a few weeks. Graduate to limited public only once you're confident in your check-in cadence. Full public release is the most consequential mode the product supports — do not use it casually.
