What happens at release

  • public_page: the release worker decrypts your bundle, publishes a landing page + signed manifest to this server's primary object storage. The URL is unguessable unless you choose to advertise it.
  • webhook: the worker POSTs a JSON payload with the public URL, manifest hash, and a detached signature over the body. Your endpoint must respond 2xx for the attempt to count.
